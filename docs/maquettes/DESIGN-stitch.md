## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful tranquility, refined botanical elegance, and effortless digital harmony. Conceived specifically for wellness immersions and nature escapes, the interface acts as a sensory buffer against digital noise, creating a calming mental state from the first millisecond of interaction.

### Aesthetic Movement & Philosophy
The style combines **Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Soft Luxury**:
- **Warm Linen Underpinnings:** Moving completely away from stark sterile whites, the surfaces use warm linen, sand, and unbleached fiber tones.
- **Botanical Grounding:** Deep, grounding forest moss and herbal sage define functional accents, conveying longevity, authenticity, and natural heritage.
- **Fluid & Pill-Soft Architecture:** Shapes borrow from polished river stones, gentle hill contours, and organic leaf curvature with generous border radiuses.
- **Uncluttered Air:** Micro-copy, breathing room, and generous safe zones simulate a mindful breath cycle, prioritizing spaciousness over density.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning individuals seeking mindful rest, wellness retreats, thermal baths, outdoor meditation, and conscious escapes.
- **Emotional Intent:** Immediate nervous system decompression, grounded clarity, trust, and refined warmth. No aggressive alerts, no garish high-saturation neon, and no high-stress micro-animations.

## Colors

The color system is strictly locked to a serene, natural light palette; dark mode is intentionally excluded to preserve the tactile warmth of unbleached linen and soft sunlight.

### Core Palette
- **Primary Canvas (`#F8F6F2`):** Soft, warm linen base for global backgrounds, page canvas, and top app bars.
- **Secondary Surface / Containers (`#E8E4DC`):** Muted stone/clay tone for cards, secondary section tiles, segmented tabs, and floating bottom sheets.
- **Brand Forest Green (`#2D5A27`):** Deep, organic leaf green serving as primary brand anchor, high-emphasis action buttons, active navigation states, and primary icons.
- **Herbal Tint / Badge Surface (`#EAF3E6`):** Delicate botanical tint for status chips, highlight tags, selection states, and wellness category pills.
- **Golden Sand / Accent (`#C3B99C`):** Subdued golden-stone beige used for subtle borders, luxury accents, ratings stars, and decorative dividers.
- **Zen Sage / Hover (`#9BB0AA`):** Muted coastal sage for interactive hover states, secondary toggles, subtle ripples, and passive indicators.
- **Deep Botanical Ink (`#1C2E1E`):** Near-black forest shade providing WCAG AAA legibility for all body copy and primary titles, without the harshness of pure black.

### Functional Roles & Contrast
- **Background:** `#F8F6F2`
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Modules):** `#E8E4DC`
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Elevated Modals / Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` with muted sand tinting
- **Text Primary:** `#1C2E1E`
- **Text Muted / Caption:** `#5A6B5C`
- **Border / Divider:** `#D8D2C5` (delicate hairlines at 0.5px to 1px)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary grace of **Playfair Display** with the geometric warmth and legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Display & Headlines
- **Playfair Display** gives editorial prestige, poise, and stillness. It is reserved for high-level screen introductions, sanctuary names, immersion titles, and welcoming narrative openers.
- Titles maintain generous line heights to prevent visual density and ensure an unhurried, graceful cadence.

### Body & Micro-Interface
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides rounded terminals, wide counters, and exceptional mobile legibility.
- It handles experiential descriptions, booking flows, interactive metadata, timestamps, and geolocation tags without feeling clinical or utilitarian.

### Uppercase & Sub-headings
- All secondary sub-headers and category eyebrows (e.g., `IMMERSIONS BIEN-ÊTRE`) utilize `label-md` or `label-sm` with letter spacing widened between `+0.04em` and `+0.08em`, echoing artisanal hospitality signage.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first around a fluid 4-column structure for smartphones, transitioning smoothly to 8 columns on tablets and a centered max-width container (480px–640px) when viewed as an installed desktop PWA.

### Rhythm & Safe Margins
- **PWA Safe Area Insets:** Margins dynamically respect native touch bounds (`env(safe-area-inset-top)` and `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), pairing with an initial top padding of `1.25rem` to prevent visual compression near hardware notches.
- **Section Pacing:** Generous vertical rhythm separates major discovery blocks with `space-xl` (`2.5rem`), encouraging intentional scrolling rather than hurried skimming.
- **Horizontal Cards & Carousels:** Experience cards feature edge-to-edge overflow scrolling with snapping points, aligned flush to the active grid margin (`margin: 1.25rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, artificial drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** combined with soft **Atmospheric Diffusions**.

### Depth Layers
- **Surface Canvas (Ground Level):** Pure `#F8F6F2`. Flat, un-shadowed, serene base.
- **Surface Cards & Trays (Level 1):** `#E8E4DC` or `#FFFFFF` containers with a hairline outline of `1px solid rgba(195, 185, 156, 0.35)`. Ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Sheets (Level 2):** `#FDFCF9` accompanied by an ultra-soft botanical ambient veil: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 46, 30, 0.08)`.
- **Translucent Top Bars & Floating Action Pills:** Frosted backdrop filter `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(248, 246, 242, 0.85)` with a delicate underside border `rgba(195, 185, 156, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design language uses **Level 3 (Pill-shaped / Ultra-Soft)** geometry, drawing direct inspiration from tumbled river stones, seed silhouettes, and organic bamboo reeds.

### Radius Architecture
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create warm, touch-friendly tap targets.
- **Immersive Visual Cards:** `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem`) to `rounded-3xl` (`1.75rem`) clipping masks for imagery, preventing sharp mechanical corners.
- **Sheets & Bottom Drawers:** Top corner radii set to `2rem` (`32px`) to cradle viewport contents gently.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** Continuous pill silhouette with inset micro-badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call to Presence):** Pill-shaped, background `#2D5A27`, foreground `#F8F6F2`, `title-sm` font. Subtle active scale feedback (`scale(0.98)`). No heavy glow; hover state softens slightly toward `#386F30`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#EAF3E6`, text `#2D5A27`, optional subtle border `1px solid rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.15)`.
- **Tertiary / Understated:** Background transparent, text `#1C2E1E`, accompanied by a minimal underline in `#C3B99C`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Default State:** Background `#E8E4DC`, text `#1C2E1E`, font `label-md`, fully rounded (`rounded-full`), padding `0.5rem 1rem`.
- **Active State:** Background `#2D5A27`, text `#F8F6F2`, softly elevated.
- **Status Badges (e.g., "Bain Sonore", "Bain de Forêt"):** Background `#EAF3E6`, text `#2D5A27`, font `label-sm`, tracking `0.05em`.

### Cards & Sanctuary Tiles
- **Structure:** Encased in `#E8E4DC` or soft white canvas `#FFFFFF`. 
- **Image Treatment:** Aspect ratios of 4:5 or 16:10 with `rounded-2xl` corners. Subtle gradient vignette `linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 60%, rgba(28,46,30,0.6) 100%)` when text overlays imagery.
- **Content Block:** Generous card padding (`space-md`), featuring Playfair Display title, nature-tag badge, and duration/price displayed in serene muted ink (`#5A6B5C`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EFE8` background, pill-rounded (`9999px`), height `48px` to `54px` for ergonomic thumb accessibility.
- **Borders:** `1px solid #D8D2C5`. Focus state transitions to `1.5px solid #2D5A27` with a soft sage ring `rgba(155, 176, 170, 0.25)`.
- **Placeholder:** `#8C998E` in `body-md`.

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Radio / Checkbox:** Organic leaf-curve or soft circular checkbox with `#2D5A27` fill and crisp `#F8F6F2` checkmark icon.
- **Toggle Switch:** Capsule track in `#D8D2C5` when inactive; smooth slide to `#2D5A27` when active. Thumb is warm unbleached stone (`#F8F6F2`).

### Bottom Navigation Bar (PWA Anchor)
- **Container:** Floating bar anchored `0.75rem` above viewport bottom with inset safe areas.
- **Styling:** Pill container, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, background `rgba(248, 246, 242, 0.92)`, bordered with `1px solid rgba(195, 185, 156, 0.4)`.
- **Icons & Labels:** Single color iconography (`#5A6B5C` inactive, `#2D5A27` active with micro-indicator dot below the leaf/zen icon).
